It's really crazy how many new software supply chain attacks via the npm Registry happened just recently. Patrick Smyth and myself presented about how the landscape is evolving and everything is accelerating at Chainguard Assemble in March with our Attacks Rewritten talk. Now video and blog post are available. We covered the various Shai Hulud waves, but Trivy, litellm, telnyx, TeamPCP, and axios all happened since then. That's crazy. 

Is it time to deactivate publishing to the npm Registry altogether for a while, and really tighten rules around new packages and scopes? Feels like it might be a good idea.
