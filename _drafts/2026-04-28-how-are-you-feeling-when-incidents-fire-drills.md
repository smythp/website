How are you feeling when incidents, fire drills, war rooms, and crisis meetings from security issues and malware are becoming a regular event? What do you think about your whole network from production systems to individual developer and user workstations and all your CI/CD pipeline on the way to production being impacted? I talked about this trend in my presentation at Chainguard Assemble with Patrick Smyth and also again last week at Google Cloud Next '26. 

Nobody wants to deal with these failures all the time. Good thing you can switch to Chainguard containers, libraries, and charts to free you. And you can also look at our new actions and skills.
