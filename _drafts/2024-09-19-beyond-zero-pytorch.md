Presented "Beyond Zero: Eliminating Vulnerabilities in the PyTorch Container Image" with [Srishti Hegde](https://www.linkedin.com/in/srishtih/) and [Dan Fernandez](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dafnz/) at the PyTorch Conference 2024 in San Francisco. The starting point: the official PyTorch image carried 1 critical, 5 high, 40 medium, and 50+ low CVEs. The talk walks through how to bring that down &mdash; stripping bloat, building fresh, going distroless, and including SBOMs.
