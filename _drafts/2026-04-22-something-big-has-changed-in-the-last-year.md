Something big has changed in the last year. We're seeing folks who would not have been able to pull off a cyberattack do cyberattacks. We're talking teenagers who have never written a line of code.

At the same time, single individuals with a technical background are pulling off attacks that look more like a sophisticated team or even an advanced persistant threat. One guy stole data and sent extortion emails to 17 organizations in one month. LLM agents were not only writing the attacks, but organizing his files, writing the emails, and picking the next target.

It's kind of like all these hackers have gone one level up in terms of threat. It's more important than ever to build on a secure foundation (* cough cough Chainguard Libraries). Check out this clip from my #Chainguard Assemble presentation on this.
